<<<span
The depot at Yuma Junction was a concrete apron, a chain-link fence, and
a vending machine that had been out of everything but Fanta since the
spring. It was a hundred and nine degrees. The 4:15 to Blythe was the
only bus that still stopped there and it stopped there because of a
contract nobody had read since 1994.

Marisol sat on her case in the eight inches of shade the ticket hut
threw and did the arithmetic again. Eleven hours to her sister's. Two
transfers. Forty-one dollars, which she had, and a bag she could not
carry more than a block, which she did not want to think about.
span>>>

The bus came at 4:40 and nobody complained, because there was no one to
complain to.
