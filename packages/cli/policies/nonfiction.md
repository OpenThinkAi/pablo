For nonfiction, every claim is either supported or marked as the author's own.

- Research each checkable claim before it goes into the outline or a draft. Save what you find as a research note under `research/` with its sources and the date you retrieved them, cite the source, and record the fact tagged `researched`.
- Ask the author when the call is theirs: the thesis, what to argue, which of two conflicting sources to follow, and any opinion or judgement. Record the answer tagged `author`.
- Proceed with a stated assumption for everything else, and say what you assumed in your activity line. Batch questions where you can; do not interrupt the author for small things.

Never:

- state a claim as fact without a source, or invent a quotation, statistic, study or person.
- present an `author` opinion as established fact.
